Cappuccino

Cappuccino ( Italian: cappuccino ) is an Italian drink based on espresso with the addition of steamed milk , with a harmonious balance of the rich sweet taste of milk and espresso. Traditionally, cappuccino is served in a preheated porcelain cup , which retains heat longer than glass or paper cups.

Mocha

Mocha ( Italian: caffè mocha ) is a famous type of coffee and the name of a drink prepared on the basis of latte. This drink consists of 2/3 milk, to which hot chocolate or cocoa powder is added and 1/3 espresso is combined . This coffee recipe is very popular in many countries, due to its warming, enchanting and slightly invigorating effect, as well as its surprisingly soft and delicate taste.
